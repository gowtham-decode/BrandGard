Evidence-led brand protection · Prototype

Meet BrandGuard.

Register official brand assets, assess suspicious submissions, and give analysts evidence they can review.

The problem

Look-alikes deserve careful review.

A similar name is a signal, not a verdict. BrandGuard compares submitted identifiers against reviewed official assets and explains the indicators behind a risk assessment.

Protect trusted assets

Record official accounts, domains, and app identifiers in a brand profile.

Explain each result

Review similarity, deceptive terms, domain signals, publisher context, and missing evidence.

Demo flow · about seven minutes

From brand profile to reviewed finding.

  • Register or sign in; show the private workspace.
  • Add a fictional brand and known official assets.
  • Record an analyst verification note for an official asset.
  • Submit a suspicious-looking social profile and app example.
  • Review evidence, uncertainty, and saved history after refresh.
How it works

Analyst input. Explainable assessment.

1 · Register

Brand profile and official asset records are scoped to the signed-in workspace.

2 · Submit

Analyst supplies profile or listing details for a scan.

3 · Assess

Similarity, normalization, domain and rule signals inform a risk tier and evidence list.

4 · Review

Analyst records a verdict; scan history persists in the configured database.

Honest prototype boundaries

Know what the demo does not claim.

  • Social profiles and Google Play listings are analyst-submitted; no social crawling or Google Play query is configured.
  • Apple catalog search can return public listing metadata; that does not verify brand ownership.
  • Google OAuth and binary logo upload are not configured.
  • Website assets support DNS TXT proof; social and app assets still require analyst review.
  • Saved source details improve traceability, but catalog selection metadata is client-reported, not chain-of-custody evidence.
Takeaway

Signals guide review.
Evidence supports decisions.

BrandGuard is a local demonstration of brand registration, submitted-signal analysis, evidence review, and persistent history — not an automated enforcement service.
